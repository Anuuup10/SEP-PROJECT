# KhanaLens

## Project Overview

KhanaLens is a mobile-first AI-powered food analysis web application.

Users can scan or upload an image of their food, and the application uses AI to identify the food items and provide estimated nutritional information.

The application is designed to help users understand what they are eating and track their nutrition over time.

## Problem Statement

Many people do not know the nutritional composition of the food they eat.

Existing calorie-tracking applications often require users to manually search for foods and enter quantities.

KhanaLens aims to simplify this process by allowing users to scan their food and receive an AI-assisted nutritional analysis.

## Main Goal

Build a simple and user-friendly web application where a user can:

1. Scan or upload food.
2. Detect individual food items using AI.
3. Estimate portions.
4. View nutritional information.
5. Save meals.
6. Track nutrition over time.

## Target Users

- Students
- Health-conscious users
- People tracking their nutrition
- People who want a quick understanding of their meals

## Core Value

Scan your food instead of manually searching for every food item.

## Important Disclaimer

Nutrition values provided by the application are estimates.

Food images cannot always determine exact ingredients, portion sizes, cooking methods, or nutritional values.

The application should not present AI estimates as medical or professionally verified nutritional advice.
